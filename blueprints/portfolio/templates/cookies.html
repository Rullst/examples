Cookies & privacy choices

We use essential security storage. This application does not install advertising or analytics trackers, so there are no optional tracking cookies to accept.

StoragePurpose and duration
rullst_csrfPrevents forged form requests. Session cookie; your browser controls when the browsing session ends or is restored.
HTTP Basic credentialsYour browser may retain the public Nexus and Studio credentials for the browsing session. They do not identify a private account.
Conversation and cloud AI choicePage memory only. Reloading clears chat history and resets cloud AI to off. HTMX history storage is disabled.

Control optional cloud AI

Each assistant starts with local replies. Select “Use cloud AI” only if you want your message sent to Groq. Uncheck it before your next message to withdraw that choice for future requests. The website remains usable without cloud AI.

Clear local data

Reload the page to clear its conversation. Use your browser's site-data controls to remove cookies; this may interrupt forms. Close the relevant browser session to clear cached Basic authentication, depending on your browser. Clearing local data does not remove server or provider records.

For server data requests, email officialrullst@gmail.com. See the privacy notice for processing, providers, and rights.
