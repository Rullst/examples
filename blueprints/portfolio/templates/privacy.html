Privacy notice

Updated September 19, 2026 · portfolio.rullst.win

Rullst is responsible for this showcase. Contact officialrullst@gmail.com for privacy questions and requests. This notice covers this portfolio and its Nexus and Studio tools. Other Rullst sites have their own notices.

A public demonstration

This is a shared developer showcase. Information entered into its CMS can be visible to everyone. Use fictional data only; do not enter private records, passwords, payment details, or children's personal information. Public demo credentials are not a private account. Demo database changes may disappear when the container restarts.

What is processed and why

  • Website delivery and security: hosting systems receive request information such as IP address, time, requested path, and browser information to serve pages and investigate errors or abuse. The site uses an essential anti-forgery cookie. Nexus and Studio use HTTP Basic authentication.
  • Public portfolio data: the profile, projects, skills, and experience are shown publicly. CMS edits are stored in the demo database until changed, removed, or the container is replaced.
  • Assistant messages: local replies are the default. Only when you select “Use cloud AI” do we send your message to Groq. Career Copilot includes the public portfolio context; the admin assistant includes a public blueprint description. Admin AI does not receive database records. Uncheck the option to keep future messages local. This cannot undo a message already sent.
  • Privacy correspondence: if you email us, we use the information you provide to answer your request. Please include only what is necessary.

Cookies, analytics, and external services

This application does not install advertising or analytics trackers. Fonts, scripts, and the default logo are served locally. External community links connect to their services when you follow them. CMS users can change public content; third-party assets they add may connect to other services.

Microsoft Azure hosts the service. Groq processes optional cloud AI requests. These providers may process data in other countries, under their own terms and applicable safeguards. See Microsoft's privacy statement and Groq's privacy policy and GroqCloud Data Processing Addendum. See our cookies and privacy choices.

Storage and retention

Chat history is held in the open page and is cleared on reload; this application does not save conversations to its database. Messages still pass through the server and, when enabled, the AI provider. Hosting logs, provider records, and privacy correspondence have separate operational retention settings; contact us for the applicable details. A container restart does not delete provider logs or correspondence.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection, and information about processing and sharing. You can withdraw consent for future optional cloud AI use. Send requests to officialrullst@gmail.com; we will assess them under the applicable law and verify identity only as necessary. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

For Brazil, these include rights under the LGPD; for the EEA and UK, rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR; and for California, applicable CCPA/CPRA rights. Other regional rights may also apply. Rullst does not use this showcase to sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and does not discriminate against people for exercising privacy rights.

Legal grounds and children

Where a legal basis is required, essential delivery and security may rely on legitimate interests, subject to applicable law and balancing of rights; optional external AI relies on your choice to consent. Legal obligations may require processing privacy requests. This developer demonstration is not intended to collect children's data. If a child has submitted personal information, contact us to request removal.

Changes

We will update this notice when the showcase's processing changes. A privacy notice alone does not certify compliance in every jurisdiction.
