This showcase uses security and functional storage. It has no advertising or audience analytics cookies configured by the application.

Security cookie

rullst_csrf is a first-party session cookie used to validate form submissions and prevent forged requests. It is not an advertising identifier. It has no persistent expiry set by the application; your browser's session restoration settings may preserve session cookies. Blocking it can prevent forms from working.

Browser and offline storage

If the showcase's service worker is installed, it may cache a small allowlist of same-origin static files to support loading assets offline. It does not cache HTML pages, Studio or Nexus responses, chat messages or form submissions. Static caches remain until replaced by a service-worker update or cleared in your browser. HTMX page-history storage is disabled to avoid saving form and chat pages there.

Cloud AI is an unchecked, per-page choice. The showcase does not store it in a tracking cookie. Uncheck it before sending another message or reload to reset it. Your browser may retain HTTP authentication credentials while you use Studio or Nexus.

Third-party resources

Some demos load fonts, scripts, styles and the logo from external technical services. Those requests disclose connection data to the service. See the privacy notice for the providers and purposes. Social links do not embed their tracking widgets in the showcase.

Your controls

You can inspect and remove this site's cookies, cache and service worker through your browser's site-data settings. Clearing them may require reloading the page or authenticating again. There are no optional tracking categories to enable here. If analytics or advertising is added later, it must remain disabled until any legally required consent has been obtained, with an equally accessible way to refuse or withdraw.
